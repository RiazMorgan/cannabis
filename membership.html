  What You Get

Every Benefit.
Explained.

Priority Drop Access

Members receive exclusive drop notifications before any public announcement. Platinum and Black members get first-access windows 48–72 hours ahead of all others.

Private Curated Delivery

All Cartel deliveries operate in full regulatory compliance. Discreet, insured, and professionally packaged in our signature matte black presentation boxes. Age verification at every drop.

Full COA Access

Every product purchase grants access to the full Certificate of Analysis from our ISO-certified lab partners. Know exactly what is in every batch — cannabinoids, terpenes, and all contaminant panels.

Harvest Event Invitations

Platinum and Black members receive curated invitations to live harvest events at partner cultivation facilities. Tour the grow, meet the cultivators, sample freshly cured cuts before they're vaulted.

Cultivator Consultations

Black tier members access direct relationships with our master cultivators. Commission custom genetic expressions, request specific terpene profiles, and contribute to the next generation of Vault cuts.

Bespoke Curation

The pinnacle of Black tier access. A dedicated council liaison curates a personalised selection from across the Vault each cycle, tailored to your documented preferences and consumption profile.
