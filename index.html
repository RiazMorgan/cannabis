  The Blackbook

Everything
You Need
to Know.

The Cartel maintains total transparency on compliance, sourcing, and membership protocols. If the Blackbook doesn't answer your question, reach out to the Council directly.

Contact the Council

Membership is by invitation or curated application. Submit your application through the portal on this page. Our council reviews applications weekly and selects based on community fit, location compliance, and referral standing. You will receive a secure communication within 7–14 business days. No walk-ins. No exceptions.

All Cartel deliveries operate under full regulatory compliance with applicable cannabis delivery licensing in each jurisdiction we serve. Age verification (21+) is required at every delivery via government-issued photo ID. We do not operate in jurisdictions where cannabis delivery is prohibited. Our legal team monitors compliance in real-time.

Every strain in the Vault has a documented genetic lineage and cultivation origin. We partner with a closed network of licensed master cultivators who operate under exclusive agreements with the Cartel. No third-party wholesale. No unknown genetics. We track provenance from seed to vault — and make all lab documentation available to members upon request.

The Cartel operates three membership tiers: Gold (curated access, standard priority), Platinum (first-access drops, dedicated liaison, private event access), and Black (all of the above plus direct cultivator relationships, bespoke curation, and council invitations). Pricing is disclosed during the application review stage. We do not publish rates publicly.

Every product in the Vault is submitted to accredited third-party ISO/IEC 17025-certified laboratories for full panel testing. This includes cannabinoid potency, terpene profile, residual solvents, pesticides, heavy metals, and microbials. Full COAs (Certificates of Analysis) are accessible via your member dashboard upon product purchase.
